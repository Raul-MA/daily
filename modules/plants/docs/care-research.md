# Plant care research

## Problem

The three additions researched on 2026-10-20 and Peperomia verde researched on 2026-11-11 need the same offline EN/ES notes, five care sections, warning signs and editable schedule defaults as the thirteen original templates. “Maranta Prayer Plant” is interpreted as *Maranta leuconeura*; “clathea lutea” and “ficus lirata” are normalized to *Calathea lutea* and *Ficus lyrata*. Cultivars were not specified. Peperomia identification remains provisional as detailed below.

## How It Works

Spanish data lives in `../src/plants/templates/`; English overlays live in `../src/plants/translations/`. The catalogue and `localized.js` register all seventeen. Selecting a template fills a draft; it does not create personal plant records automatically.

Winter guidance protects from cold glass and drafts; feeding follows actual spring/summer growth. No live weather integration or automatic watering is added.

## Gotchas

- The existing form expresses Northern Hemisphere watering seasons as Mar–Sep/Oct–Feb and fertilizer seasons as Mar–Aug/Sep–Feb. The first three additions retain equal provisional checks; Peperomia uses distinct provisional warm/cool checks. Neither establishes validated seasonal watering frequencies. Climate, indoor light and actual growth take precedence.
- Feeding sources differ: RHS recommends monthly Maranta feeding, while NYBG recommends fortnightly during growth. The Maranta default uses RHS monthly guidance. RHS general calathea feeding is Apr–Sep; the app's Mar–Aug window is a disclosed seasonal adaptation. Skip feeding outside active growth and follow product directions.
- Localized unit coverage checks all seventeen guides, translated numeric figures and identical schedules while retaining the original thirteen-template assertion on its original subset and the previous sixteen-template subset count. Existing browser coverage also selects all four additions and checks populated schedules.
